## Maintaining the catalog

For additions, establish the official ministry source and exact video, check the named passage and relevant content, disclose theological perspective, review credible reporting of serious conduct concerns, and record the review date and limitations. Do not silently substitute algorithmic recommendations. `approved` means selected for this app under these editorial criteria, not personal endorsement by the owner or verification by YouTube. Set it false to remove a recommendation. Recheck before public release and when a concern or broken link is reported.

Thumbnails load from YouTube’s image host; playback occurs on YouTube only after following the link. Images are not copied into downloads or share cards. Text remains usable if an image fails. No autoplay or iframe players are included in this app.
