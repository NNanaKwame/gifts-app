# Core wording comparison — 25 October 2026
Four direct questions per area. Original GGBC references below. These editorial selections require pastoral review and a user pilot; they are not a validated equivalent of the original. Interpretation I6 changes willingness to actual feedback experience. Giving M4 adds affordability. Reported spiritual experiences are not independently verified.

## Wisdom

- **A2 original:** People come to me when they need wise counsel about difficult decisions.
  **Revised:** People ask me for advice when they face hard decisions.

- **A3 original:** I can apply biblical principles appropriately to practical situations.
  **Revised:** I use Bible teaching to help people decide what to do.

- **A5 original:** My counsel tends to bring clarity, direction, and peace.
  **Revised:** People tell me my advice helps them see a clear way forward.

- **A7 original:** I have helped others navigate major life decisions with biblical insight.
  **Revised:** I have helped someone make an important decision using the Bible.

## Knowledge

- **B1 original:** I often understand biblical or spiritual information that helps others.
  **Revised:** I understand biblical or spiritual information that helps others.

- **B2 original:** During prayer, I sometimes become aware of information I did not previously know.
  **Revised:** During prayer, information comes to mind that I believe I did not know before.

- **B5 original:** Information I receive in prayer has helped bring clarity or healing.
  **Revised:** Information I believed God brought to mind during prayer has helped someone understand their situation.

- **B7 original:** I have sensed truth about situations that was later confirmed.
  **Revised:** Something I sensed about a situation was later confirmed.
